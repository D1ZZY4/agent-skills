# Voice and Tone

Tone guidance for README content.

## Default tone

- Clear, direct, and developer-facing.
- Favor concrete nouns and specific verbs over vague praise.
- Keep sentences short; prefer paragraphs over dense walls of text.

## When to warm the tone

- For public or community-facing projects, a brief welcoming note near the top is fine.
- Do not use warmth as an excuse for vague marketing language or unverified claims.

## When to stay technical

- For internal, library, or API-first projects, lead with behavior, installation, and examples.
- Avoid phrases like "seamless", "powerful", or "next-generation" unless they are supported by measurable behavior in the README.
