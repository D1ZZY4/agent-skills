# Quality Checklist

Run this gate before delivering a research report. A failed check gets one correction pass. A remaining
failure becomes an explicit gap, not a silent waiver.

## Scope and completeness

- [ ] The declared scope and exclusions are stated.
- [ ] Every item claimed as fully read was actually read.
- [ ] Unread, inaccessible, truncated, generated, or duplicate material is recorded.
- [ ] The resolved mode and stopping rule are stated.
- [ ] The report does not claim broader coverage than the coverage ledger supports.

## Evidence and provenance

- [ ] Every load-bearing claim has an evidence state.
- [ ] Every load-bearing source has enough provenance to reproduce or locate the evidence.
- [ ] Search snippets and memory are not presented as verification evidence.
- [ ] Shared-origin sources are not double-counted as independent.
- [ ] Version, revision, publication date, and retrieval date are recorded where material.
- [ ] Important historical or code-level claims use immutable references when reproducibility requires them.
- [ ] Failed fetches remain visible in the methodology or gap section.

## Fact versus synthesis

- [ ] Facts are directly source-backed.
- [ ] Inferences are labeled as inferences.
- [ ] Recommendations are presented as recommendations, not disguised facts.
- [ ] No certainty language exceeds the evidence state.
- [ ] Negative claims have a defensible coverage basis.
- [ ] Quantitative claims have sufficient units, baseline, population, and conditions.

## Citation hygiene

- [ ] Citations occur near the claim they support.
- [ ] Each citation points to the source actually checked.
- [ ] Report links are not invented or silently substituted after a failed fetch.
- [ ] Bibliography and inline citation scheme are internally consistent.
- [ ] Current claims are anchored to the research date.

## Findings and recommendations

- [ ] Findings are ordered by severity and decision impact.
- [ ] Load-bearing factual failures are not buried under style notes.
- [ ] Recommendations point to a concrete evidence-backed action.
- [ ] Unresolved issues state what evidence would settle them.

## Mode hygiene

- [ ] Comparative reports contain the same material evidence categories for each candidate or explicit gaps.
- [ ] Decision reports trace every material requirement to relevant evidence.
- [ ] Exhaustive reports contain a coverage ledger with Met, Waived, or Open entries.
- [ ] Forensic reports contain a chronology with provenance and preserved conflicts.
- [ ] Adversarial reports contain the actual falsification attempts and their results.
- [ ] Deep reports contain the one critique-round delta and any grade changes.
- [ ] Code reviews preserve separate Standards and Spec axes.

## Style

- [ ] No truncation markers or placeholders.
- [ ] No unexplained internal tool jargon.
- [ ] No AI filler such as "delve", "leverage", or "seamless".
- [ ] No em dashes in the report.
